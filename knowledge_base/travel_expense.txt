Business travel should be approved by the employee’s manager before bookings are made. The request should state the purpose, destination, dates, and expected expenses.

Employees should use approved booking channels and choose reasonable travel options. Any exception to the travel guidelines should receive prior authorization where possible.

Receipts and invoices should be collected for eligible travel expenses. Claims should include the business purpose, date, amount, and required supporting documents.

Expense claims must be submitted through the approved reimbursement process within the stated submission period. Personal expenses and unsupported claims may be excluded.

The manager and finance team review claims against the applicable policy and available approvals. Reimbursements are processed after the claim is verified and approved.
