Employees should inform their manager if a medical issue affects their ability to work or attend the workplace. Personal medical information should be shared only with authorized personnel when necessary.

Medical leave requests must follow the organization’s leave application process. A medical certificate may be required depending on the duration and applicable rules.

Employees who need workplace adjustments should contact HR to discuss suitable arrangements. Requests are reviewed with due regard for privacy and operational requirements.

In a workplace emergency, contact the designated emergency response team or local emergency services. Do not delay urgent medical attention while completing administrative formalities.

Any available health insurance or reimbursement benefit is subject to the applicable plan terms. Employees should consult HR or the insurer for coverage, claim documents, and deadlines.
