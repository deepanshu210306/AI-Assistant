The hiring process begins when a department submits an approved role request. The request should include the job title, responsibilities, required skills, and reporting manager.

The HR team publishes the vacancy on approved channels and collects applications. Applicants are shortlisted against the role requirements and relevant experience.

Shortlisted candidates are invited to complete the required assessments or technical screening. Assessment criteria should be consistent for candidates applying to the same role.

Candidates who pass screening attend interview rounds with the hiring team. Interviewers submit their feedback promptly using the standard evaluation format.

HR coordinates reference checks, approvals, and the final offer. The selected candidate receives joining instructions after accepting the offer.
