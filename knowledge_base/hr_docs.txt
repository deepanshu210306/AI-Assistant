Employees should keep their personal and contact information updated with the HR team. Changes to bank details, address, or emergency contacts should be reported through the approved process.

The organization maintains employee records for employment administration and statutory requirements. Access to these records is limited to authorized personnel.

Employees should follow the organization’s code of conduct and workplace communication guidelines. Concerns about workplace behaviour may be raised with HR or the designated reporting contact.

New employees complete onboarding activities, including document submission and an introduction to company policies. Managers should clarify role expectations and initial priorities.

Employment letters, experience letters, and other HR documents can be requested through the HR helpdesk. Requests are processed after identity and record details are verified.
