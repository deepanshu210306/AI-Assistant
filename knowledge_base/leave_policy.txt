Employees should submit planned leave requests through the approved leave management system. Requests should be made in advance so managers can plan team coverage.

Leave approval depends on the available balance, team requirements, and the applicable leave category. Employees should wait for confirmation before treating planned leave as approved.

For unexpected illness or emergencies, employees should inform their manager as soon as reasonably possible. Supporting documents may be requested where required by policy.

Public holidays and weekly offs are handled according to the organization’s published holiday calendar. Any work scheduled on these days should be agreed with the manager in advance.

Leave balances and request status can be checked in the leave management system. Questions about eligibility, corrections, or special cases should be directed to HR.
