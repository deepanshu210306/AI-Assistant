A Root Cause Analysis (RCA) is used to understand why an incident or failure occurred. The objective is to identify contributing causes and prevent recurrence rather than assign blame.

The incident owner should record the date, impact, affected systems or teams, and a clear description of the event. Relevant logs, reports, and supporting evidence should be preserved.

The team reviews the sequence of events and identifies immediate triggers as well as underlying process or system weaknesses. Conclusions should be supported by evidence.

Corrective actions address the current issue, while preventive actions reduce the chance of similar incidents. Each action should have an owner and a target completion date.

The RCA is reviewed by the relevant stakeholders and stored in the approved knowledge repository. Action owners should provide updates until all agreed actions are closed.
